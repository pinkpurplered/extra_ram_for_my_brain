# Privacy Policy — Recall Audio

**Last updated:** August 17, 2026

Recall Audio ("the app") is developed by Sin Yu Ting. This policy explains what data the app handles.

## Summary

Recall Audio does **not** collect, transmit, or sell your personal data to us or third-party analytics or advertising services. Audio is recorded and stored on your device, and saved clips may sync to your personal iCloud account if you are signed in.

## Microphone

The app uses the microphone to record audio into a rolling on-device buffer (up to one hour). When you save a clip, it is written as an audio file. Audio is not uploaded to our servers.

## Data stored on your device and in your iCloud account

- Rolling audio segments (the temporary buffer) stay on your iPhone
- Saved clips (`.m4a` files) and folder names you create
- If you are signed in to iCloud with iCloud Drive enabled, saved clips are stored in your iCloud Drive container for Recall Audio and may sync across your devices signed into the same Apple ID

We do not have access to your recordings or iCloud data.

## Third-party services

The app does not include analytics, advertising, or third-party tracking SDKs.

## Children's privacy

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from children.

## Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above will change when we do.
