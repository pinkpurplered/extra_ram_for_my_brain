# Privacy Policy — Recall Audio

**Last updated:** August 17, 2026

Recall Audio ("the app") is developed by Sin Yu Ting. This policy explains what data the app handles.

## Summary

Recall Audio does **not** collect, transmit, or sell your personal data. Audio is processed and stored on your iPhone unless you choose to export it.

## Microphone

The app uses the microphone to record audio into a rolling on-device buffer (up to one hour). Saved clips are stored locally in the app's documents folder. Audio is not uploaded to our servers.

## Data stored on your device

- Rolling audio segments and saved clips (`.m4a` files)
- Folder and clip names you create in the app

This data stays on your device unless you back up your iPhone through iCloud or a computer backup, or export files via the Files app.

## Third-party services

The app does not include analytics, advertising, or third-party tracking SDKs.

## Children's privacy

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from children.

## Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above will change when we do.
