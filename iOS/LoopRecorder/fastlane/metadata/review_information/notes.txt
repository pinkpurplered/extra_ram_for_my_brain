Recall Audio is a background rolling audio recorder for iPhone.

How to test:
1. Use a physical iPhone (microphone required; simulator is not supported).
2. Sign in to iCloud and enable iCloud Drive in Settings (recommended so saved clips appear under Files → iCloud → Recall Audio).
3. Launch the app and tap the microphone button to start recording.
4. Wait at least 1–2 minutes, then use the slider and tap "Save last X minutes".
5. Open the list icon (top right) to preview saved clips, or open Files → iCloud → Recall Audio (or On My iPhone → Recall Audio if iCloud is unavailable).
6. The orange/green microphone indicator appears in the status bar while recording, including in the background.

No login, account, or demo credentials are required. Saved clips stay on the user's device or personal iCloud account; we do not operate servers that receive audio.
